Mantis Gardens / HQ Internal Tools

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026  ·  Mantis Gardens LLC

Mantis Gardens HQ is an internal business operations tool used exclusively by Mantis Gardens LLC staff. It is not a public application and does not collect data from the general public.

1. About This Application

Mantis Gardens HQ ("the App") is a private web application built for internal use by Mantis Gardens LLC, a landscaping business based in Sacramento, California. The App helps the business manage crew scheduling, client records, work documentation, and invoicing.

Access is restricted to authorized employees and the business owner. No data from third parties, customers of third parties, or the general public is collected through this App.

2. Data We Collect

The App collects and stores only data directly entered by authorized Mantis Gardens LLC staff:

  • Client information — names, addresses, phone numbers, and service notes for Mantis Gardens landscaping clients
  • Work records — service dates, crew names, materials used, and service notes entered after each job
  • Schedule data — read from the business's Google Calendar; no calendar data is modified by this App
  • Authentication data — Google account email addresses of authorized staff, verified via Google Sign-In

3. How We Use Data

All data is used solely to operate the business functions of Mantis Gardens LLC:

  • Displaying crew schedules and job assignments
  • Creating and storing work record documentation in Google Drive
  • Generating draft invoices in QuickBooks Online
  • Maintaining client service history

Data is never sold, shared with third parties for marketing purposes, or used for any purpose outside the internal operation of Mantis Gardens LLC.

4. QuickBooks Integration

The App connects to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit OAuth 2.0 API to create draft invoices on behalf of the business owner. This integration:

  • Operates under the QuickBooks account of Mantis Gardens LLC only
  • Creates draft invoices using client and work record data already stored in the App
  • Does not access, read, or modify any QuickBooks data beyond creating invoices
  • Uses OAuth tokens stored securely in Google Apps Script Script Properties, accessible only to the Apps Script project owner

5. Data Storage

All data is stored within the Google Workspace account of Mantis Gardens LLC (mantisgardensca@gmail.com) using Google Sheets and Google Drive. No external databases or third-party data stores are used. Google's own privacy policy governs the storage of data within Google Workspace.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as it is needed for business operations. Client records and work history are retained indefinitely for service continuity. Staff login logs are periodically cleared by the business owner.

7. Your Rights

As this App is restricted to Mantis Gardens LLC staff, data rights are governed by the employment relationship with Mantis Gardens LLC. Clients of Mantis Gardens whose information is stored in the App may request access to or deletion of their data by contacting Mantis Gardens directly.
